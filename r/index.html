How to use it

  1. Install Cook What? from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Open the app and tap "Set up a household".
  3. Type the code above in "Partner code", then create your household.
  4. Start your 14-day free trial from the app.

Already signed up but not started a trial yet? Tap "Have a partner code?" on the free-trial screen and enter it there.

The 14-day free trial is for the household owner's first trial. Your App Store or Google Play account is asked for a payment method at the start; after 14 days the subscription renews monthly at S$7.98 until you cancel, which you can do any time in your store's subscription settings. If your store account has already had a Cook What? free trial, the plan starts without one.
